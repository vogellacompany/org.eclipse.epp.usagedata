Usage Data Collector Terms of Use

This document describes what the Usage Data Collector records and what happens to the data you choose to upload.

  • A unique id is generated for every workstation and for every workspace that submits data. These ids are random, contain nothing about you, and are transmitted with every upload so that uploads from the same installation can be told apart from others.
  • The Usage Data Collector is not intended to collect information that identifies you as an individual. It may inadvertently pick up such information where it appears in plug-in names.
  • The data relates to how you use the application and the plug-ins installed in it: plug-in names and versions, the time spent in a specific editor, view or perspective, the actions and commands you invoked, and similar events. Think of it as the "click stream" that some web sites record.

Uses of the Information

  • The data is sent to the upload location configured on the "Usage Data Collector | Uploading" preferences page. The organization that operates that location receives the data in its collected form and may use it, in raw or aggregated form, to understand how the application and its plug-ins are used and how many people use them.
  • Ask the operator of the configured upload location how the data is stored, for how long, and with whom it is shared.

Interactions with the Usage Data Collector

  • With your permission, the Usage Data Collector periodically transmits your usage data. The first time it attempts to do so, you are asked for permission. If you decline, the collector stops until you enable it again. If you agree, you can let it upload automatically without further interaction, or have it ask you before every upload.
  • You can limit what is recorded. The plug-ins can be restricted to those from the Eclipse namespace, plug-ins whose name contains a given text can be excluded, and bundle lifecycle events are left out by default. The filters are on the "Usage Data Collector" preferences pages.
  • At any time, you may view the data that has been collected on the "Usage Data Collector | Preview" preferences page.
  • You may stop collecting at any time by turning off the "Enable Capture" checkbox on the "Usage Data Collector" preferences page.

More Information

The source code, documentation and issue tracker of the Usage Data Collector are at https://github.com/vogellacompany/org.eclipse.epp.usagedata.
